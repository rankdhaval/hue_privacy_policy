Privacy Policy – Hue Drop

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Hue Drop (package com.shreehari.huedrop), published by Shreehari ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the game.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (best depth, statistics, gems, settings such as sound and music volume) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hue Drop is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location
  • Interactions with the app and ads (for example app launches, taps and video views)
  • Diagnostic information (such as app performance and crash data)

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Settings inside the game.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Sharing

Apart from the advertising data described above that is processed by Google AdMob, we do not sell or share your personal information with anyone.

4. Children

Hue Drop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the game. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies. For questions about deletion, contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We rely on industry-standard protections provided by Android and Google, including encryption of data in transit.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
